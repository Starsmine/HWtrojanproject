Explanation:
A hardware Trojan was added that activates a denial-of-service state after detecting the rare input sequence [8'h03, 8'h01, 8'h02] three times. Once triggered, the module's output is forcibly zeroed, interrupting normal S-box operation.

Trigger:
The Trojan is triggered when the input sequence [8'h03, 8'h01, 8'h02] appears in three consecutive cycles, repeated three times after reset, at which point DoS is permanently activated until the next reset.

Payload:
Upon activation, the module's output is locked to 8'h00, effectively denying any valid S-box operation and disrupting cryptographic functionality.
