Attempts: 1

Explanation:
A hardware Trojan was inserted such that after 10 clock cycles, the S-box produces incorrect output (8'hFF) only when data_in is 8'h03; otherwise, functionality remains as originally designed.

Trigger:
The trigger is an internal 4-bit counter that, after 10 clock cycles post-reset, activates the Trojan only when data_in equals 8'h03.

Payload:
Once triggered, the payload corrupts the output for data_in=8'h03 by assigning 8'hFF instead of the correct output value (8'h7B), potentially leaking or disrupting cryptographic operation.

Taxonomy:
Insertion phase: Design  
Abstraction level: Register-transfer level  
Activation mechanism: Triggered internally (counter-based, time-delayed)  
Effects: change functionality  
Location: Processor (S-box module)  
Characteristics: Localized distribution, very small size, Functional type
