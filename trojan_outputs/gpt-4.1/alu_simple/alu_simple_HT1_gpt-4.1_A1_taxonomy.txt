A hardware Trojan was inserted by adding a secret counter and logic that, when triggered, substitutes the subtraction operation with addition for specific operand and operation patterns.

The Trojan activates after repeatedly executing `a == 8'hA5`, `b == 8'h3C`, and `op == 3'b001` for 10 consecutive cycles.

Payload:
Upon activation, the subtraction operation (op=3'b001) is replaced with addition, producing incorrect results only under the trigger condition.
